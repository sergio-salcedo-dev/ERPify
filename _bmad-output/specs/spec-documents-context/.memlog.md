- (decision) D1/D2 upload transport (user): evidence is uploaded in two steps. Step 1 declares size (and optionally sha256), carries the actor-scoped idempotency key and creates the upload-intent record; a zero or over-MAX_ORIGINAL_BYTES declared size is refused there, before any keystore row, so it leaves no tombstone. Step 2 is a raw-body PUT sealed as it is read; Content-Length is mandatory (411 otherwise) and must equal the declared size, and the bytes counted at end of stream must equal it, else the upload falls into the aborted-upload class. No unknown-length stream exists, so a body Caddy cuts off can never read as a complete shorter original, and no multipart UploadedFile spool exists for evidence.

- (decision) D3 scan retry (user, narrows the earlier 'unavailable burns no attempt'): unavailable means only 'before any content was sent'.
