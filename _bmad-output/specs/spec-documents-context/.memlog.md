- (decision) Documents owns evidence: the original byte is immutable while it exists, never transformed; derivatives are produced alongside it (ADR D1, D7)
- (capability) CAP-1 Ingest evidence untransformed: UploadEvidence streams the original to Documents' own storage port; digest, actor, date, legal basis and retention policy are recorded; success: stored bytes equal uploaded bytes, digest matches
- (capability) CAP-2 Readiness gate: a document is consumable only once scanned and ready; scanning classifies, it never cleans; success: a read before ready is refused, an infected or unscanned one never reaches a consumer
- (capability) CAP-3 Sealed at rest: originals are encrypted by a stream-oriented sibling of EnvelopeEncryptor under a per-document EncryptionScopeId; success: a multi-gigabyte upload is sealed without buffering it in memory and decrypts back byte-identical
- (capability) CAP-4 Erase by key destruction: destroyScope() ends the conservation contract while digest and custody chain survive; success: after erasure the content cannot be decrypted and digest plus custody remain readable
- (capability) CAP-5 Dependent derivatives die with their origin: Documents materialises previews through the images module (entry point three, origin recorded) and orders delete(ImageId) on erasure; success: after erasure no derivative of that document is retrievable by any route, cached URLs included
- (capability) CAP-6 Document metadata and custody trail: each state change of a document is auditable; success: the sequence of states of one document can be listed in order
- (capability) CAP-7 Versioning: a new version is a new immutable original linked to its predecessor, never a modification; success: both versions retrievable with distinct digests until erased
- (capability) CAP-8 Searchable scanned PDFs (OCR): text extracted from scanned PDFs becomes a searchable derivative; success: a phrase present only in a scanned page is found
- (constraint) Symfony 8.1 argument resolver: the upload seam is designed against mergeParamsAndFiles from the start; Shared/Http/Infrastructure/TransportOnlyUploadedFileDenormalizer is the existing guard, not re-derived; neither transport types nor caller-supplied paths, filenames or URLs reach Application (ADR invariant 6)
- (constraint) Documents gets its OWN storage port with stream writes and sealing; no generic port spans it and ImageStorage (ADR D7); the withdrawn shared StoragePort is what blurred the boundary
- (constraint) No promotion between contracts: a fungible image never becomes evidence; the supported path is re-supplying the original through UploadEvidence (ADR D3)
- (constraint) The use case chooses the contract (UploadEvidence vs UploadImage), never a heuristic over MIME or size; interim rule stands until Documents ships: no surface accepts an evidence-class binary through the images pipeline
